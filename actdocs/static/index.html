What is it?

The London Perl & Raku Workshop (LPW) is a free one-day technical conference in Central London, United Kingdom.

  • WHEN: Saturday 21 November 2026. Arrive from 09:00 for a 9:20 start
  • WHERE: The Café at Zoopla, The Cooperage, 5 Copper Row, London SE1 2LH (more detail)

About

Run by volunteers from the London Perl & Raku community, LPW will have talks about Perl, Raku, and other languages, technologies, and topics.

LPW is an inclusive event and we would love to have you attend, whether this is your first time learning about Perl or Raku, or your 18th workshop. We encourage attendees and speakers of any age, background or circumstance, and we are open to receiving proposals for talks on any subject you think your fellow attendees will find interesting.

Further news will be posted here and to our feed.

How can I attend?

To attend is free, all you need to do is create a new user account or log in and then register. We encourage all attendees to register in advance for easier access to the venue.

Sponsors

LPW is reliant on the generosity of our sponsors to run a great community event at no cost to our attendees. If you or your company would like to sponsor the London Perl & Raku Workshop then please see our sponsorship information and do not hesitate to contact the organisers if you have any questions.

We also greatly appreciate donations from attendees.
